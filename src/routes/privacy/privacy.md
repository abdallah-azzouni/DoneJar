## 3. What data we collect and why

### 3.1 Account data

When you sign up, we collect your **email address** and a **hashed password**. We use this to authenticate you and to restore your account. We do not collect your name, phone number, or any other personal detail unless you choose to provide it.

**Legal basis (GDPR):** Contract — this data is necessary to provide the service.

### 3.2 Your content

DoneJar stores the tasks, notes, columns, projects, and file attachments you create. This content belongs to you. We store it in order to sync it across your devices and keep it available when you return.

**Legal basis (GDPR):** Contract.

### 3.3 Error and session data (Sentry)

We use **Sentry** (sentry.io) to monitor errors and diagnose bugs. When an error occurs, Sentry automatically captures information about the browser environment, the JavaScript call stack, and a session replay — a recording of DOM interactions in your browser (not a screen recording). By default, Sentry masks all text content and user input before it leaves your browser, so the content of your notes is not visible in these replays. Sentry may capture your rough IP address as part of error events.

We use session replay data only to fix bugs and improve reliability. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

**Legal basis (GDPR):** Legitimate interest — diagnosing and fixing technical issues.

### 3.4 Analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

DoneJar is hosted on **Cloudflare Pages**, which provides us with aggregate traffic analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, does not store data in your browser, and does not fingerprint or track individual users. We receive aggregate data such as page views, referrers, and country-level traffic — nothing tied to you personally.

**Legal basis (GDPR):** Legitimate interest — understanding how the product is being used.

### 3.5 Cookies and local storage

We do not use tracking cookies. We may use your browser's local storage or IndexedDB to cache your data locally as part of DoneJar's local-first architecture. This data stays on your device and is not used to track you across other websites.

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not set any cookies. Sentry may set a minimal session identifier cookie to link error events within a single session; no cross-site tracking is involved.

If you are located in the EU or Qatar, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your region, including the United States (Sentry) and globally (Cloudflare CDN). We take steps to ensure these transfers are protected:

- **Supabase** stores your data in the EU (Ireland region).

- **Sentry** is SOC 2 Type II certified and compliant with applicable data transfer frameworks.

- **Cloudflare** processes only aggregate, non-personal analytics data.

## 6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and content data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently and immediately deleted from our systems upon account deletion.

Error logs in Sentry are retained according to Sentry's standard retention policies (typically 90 days).

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data we hold about you

- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate data

- **Deletion** — request deletion of your account and all associated data (available directly in the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account)

- **Restriction** — request that we restrict processing of your data

- **Portability** — request your data in a machine-readable format

- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **a.azzouni25@gmail.com**. We will respond within 30 days.

**Qatar users:** Your rights are protected under Qatar's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 13 of 2016) and its associated regulatory guidelines.

**EU/EEA users:** Your rights are protected under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you believe we have not handled your data appropriately, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

- Passwords are stored in hashed form and never in plain text

- All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS

- Access to production systems is restricted to the operator

- Row-level security policies are enforced on the database

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.
